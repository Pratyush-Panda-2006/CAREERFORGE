Engine contract & limits
The actor selector represents a trusted caller supplying an identity; it is not identity verification. Policies contain additive allow rules. Parent grants are combined with bitwise OR; a wildcard is expanded against the fixed action registry, never evaluated as code. Removing a direct grant cannot cancel an inherited grant. This is not an explicit-deny policy language.
Bits 0–6 follow the displayed action order. Mask 17 requires catalog read and inventory write; zero requests no permissions. One-argument evaluatePolicyBitmask(mask) evaluates the customer role. Users are bounded at 32; the four roles and bootstrap owner are fixed. Draft policy input is limited to 2 KB. Nothing is stored, uploaded or applied to real orders. Refresh resets all changes.
