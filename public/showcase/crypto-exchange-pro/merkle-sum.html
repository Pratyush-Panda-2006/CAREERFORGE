Commitment rules and limits

Balances use unsigned 128-bit units at a fixed scale of 10⁸. Inputs accept 0–8 decimal places, no exponent, sign, whitespace, or leading zeroes. The aggregate has the same bound. Each account is unique after trimming; at most 1,024 leaves are accepted.

Leaves hash the JSON tuple ["merkle-sum-v1","leaf",account,integerUnits]. Parents hash ["merkle-sum-v1","branch",leftHash,leftSum,rightHash,rightSum]. Odd nodes are promoted unchanged. A singleton root is its leaf, with an empty proof. No balances are duplicated.
