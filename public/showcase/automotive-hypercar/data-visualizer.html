How the area is calculated

Scores scale each axis radius linearly. Vertices start at the top and proceed clockwise at equal angles. The shoelace formula measures the enclosed polygon in SVG square units. Coverage compares it with the all-100 polygon on this exact grid. Axis order affects area; this geometric measure is not an overall performance rating or a safety recommendation.
