Model assumptions and timing

1,800 kg point mass, 700 kW available power, 115 m/s speed cap; no elevation, downforce, traffic or temperature model. A backward braking pass and forward traction/power pass solve 25 m or shorter segments. Corner limits use v² = μgr; compound grip decreases with tire age and distance. Soft slicks have more dry grip and faster wear, while wet tires have more wet grip. Playback uses elapsed timestamps and pauses when the tab is hidden. Model assumptions

A 1,950 kg, 2.8 m wheelbase vehicle is modeled with a linear bicycle chassis, bounded tire forces, and a proportional yaw-rate controller. Torque is split across four 500 Nm wheel motors; differential wheel torque supplies the corrective yaw moment. Tire colors show estimated combined lateral and longitudinal grip utilization. Values are illustrative and are not suitable for real vehicle setup or safety decisions.
