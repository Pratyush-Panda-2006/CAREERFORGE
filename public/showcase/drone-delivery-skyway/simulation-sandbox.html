What this model assumes

Each flight time is baseline × (1 + variation × a standard normal draw), with a one-minute minimum. Flights are independent. Inputs are hypothetical; calibrating them requires observed delivery data.

The shaded band describes individual simulated outcomes. The Wilson interval describes sampling uncertainty in the late-outcome share. It does not include uncertainty in the model or its inputs.
