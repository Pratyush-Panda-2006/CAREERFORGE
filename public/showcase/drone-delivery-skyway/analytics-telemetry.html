What the report contains

Fixed target names, coarse pointer cells, scroll depth in five-percent steps and relative active time. Form values, page URLs, credentials, browser fingerprints and persistent identifiers are excluded.

Pausing collection or hiding the tab pauses its clock. A report is created only when you request a download. No measurements are sent over the network or written to browser storage.

History retains the latest 100 events; aggregate counters retain the session totals up to one million clicks. Active time is capped at 24 hours.
