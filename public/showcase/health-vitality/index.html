Your body transitions across distinct metabolic phases during a fast:

Stage 1 (0 - 4 hrs) Anabolic State Completed

Blood sugar & insulin levels elevate. Food absorption and cellular nourishment occurring.

Insulin drops, digestive tract rests. Liver converts stored glycogen into steady blood glucose.

Stage 3 (12 - 18 hrs) Fat Burning (Lipolysis) Current Stage

Glycogen stores depleted. Body oxidizes stored fatty acids for primary cellular fuel. Human Growth Hormone (HGH) surges.

Stage 4 (18+ hrs) Ketosis & Autophagy Locked

Liver generates ketone bodies (BHB). Deep cellular recycling (autophagy) breaks down damaged cellular proteins.
