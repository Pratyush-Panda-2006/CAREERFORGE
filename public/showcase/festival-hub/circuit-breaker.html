    The recovery contract

    Open circuits reject calls without invoking the request function. After the cooldown, exactly one half-open recovery trial is admitted. A successful trial closes the circuit and resets backoff. A failed trial doubles the cooldown from 800 ms up to 6,400 ms. Restart cooldown retains the current delay and cannot bypass it.

    Trip, reset and page exit cancel in-flight work; late results cannot restore an obsolete state. Requests receive an AbortSignal and must cooperate to stop their own external work. The breaker bounds waiting and ignores late completions even when a callback ignores cancellation. At most 32 requests can be active. Reset or returning from the back-forward cache starts a fresh local demonstration.
