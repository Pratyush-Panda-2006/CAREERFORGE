        TERRAINITIA / ECOLOGY LAB

        Ecological Resurgence Simulator

        Explore ten illustrative years of vegetation and herbivore interaction. Flora is the resource and wildlife is its consumer in this simplified Lotka–Volterra model. Values are abundance indices, not measured species counts, hectares, or a conservation forecast.

        ━ Flora┄ Wildlife

        Annual values and model assumptions
        Illustrative abundance indices; final partial year included if the solver stops early.
        YearFloraWildlife

        dF/dt = αF − βFW; dW/dt = δFW − γW. Rates are constant and the model omits carrying capacity, migration, weather, and human intervention. Zero populations remain absent. Classical cycles are not proof of a recovering ecosystem.

        Fixed fourth-order Runge–Kutta steps of 1/240 year, sampled monthly. A trajectory outside the finite 0–1,000,000 display range stops visibly instead of clipping its values. Model background: University of Florida biological physics.
