Endpoint

Application heartbeat: this client sends __quantum_ping__ every 2 seconds; your server must reply __quantum_pong__ within 1.5 seconds. These are text messages, not WebSocket control-frame pings. An HTTPS page may require a secure wss:// endpoint.

Message flow

Up to 50 offline messages, 4,096 characters each. FIFO flush after reconnect. Local sends are not server acknowledgements; no exactly-once delivery guarantee. Disconnect clears the queue; retry exhaustion retains it for an explicit retry.

Connection timeline

Newest first · last 100 events · no persistence. At most 5 consecutive failed retries; success resets the retry count. Backoff grows from 250 ms to a 5-second ceiling with 50–100% jitter.
