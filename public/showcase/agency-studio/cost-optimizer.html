The complete demo model

A compute unit represents a worker-hour, a storage unit one GB-month, and a bandwidth unit one GB transferred. One demo credit has 100 integer subunits. The first tier is full price; only units in the second and third tiers receive 10% and 20% discounts. Earlier units are never repriced. Discounts round half up to a subunit per resource tier.
